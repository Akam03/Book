My dearest Cheena,

This is placeholder text for the closing letter, the part of the book where the narrator stops telling the story and just talks to you.

If you've reached this page, you've read the first nine months of us. Some of it you lived alongside me, and some of it, maybe, you're only hearing now: what I was thinking, what I was too shy to say, what I felt long before I said it out loud.

Thank you for every message, every late night, every "one more thing" that turned into an hour. Thank you for being patient with me on the hard days, and for making the ordinary days feel like something worth writing down.

You love stories more than anyone I know. I hope you like this one, because I intend to keep writing it with you for a very long time.
