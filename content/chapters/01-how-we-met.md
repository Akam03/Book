It started the way the best stories do: quietly, without either of us knowing that we had just turned to the first page. It was March, and the world was doing its usual ordinary things, and then there was you.

This is placeholder text, my love. Akam will replace it with the real story, every word of it, but for now imagine the first message, the nervous pause before pressing send, and the way a single reply can change the shape of an entire year.

We talked that first night until it was far too late to call it night anymore. I remember thinking that I should say goodnight, and then thinking of one more thing to tell you, and then one more after that. Some conversations end. Ours just kept turning into the next one.

By the end of the first week, talking to you had become the best part of every day. By the end of the second, I had stopped pretending it was anything else.

There's a version of my life where I never sent that first hello. I think about it sometimes, the way you think about a road you almost took, and I'm so glad I'll never know what it looks like.

Every day after that, without fail, we found something to say. Small things and big things, silly things and the kind of things you only tell someone once you trust them. It was March, then it was April, and somewhere in between, you became my favourite person to talk to.
