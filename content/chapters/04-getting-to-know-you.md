Somewhere along the way, your name stopped being enough, and we started collecting new ones.

You became ==Nona== first. Then ==baby==, of course, and ==sweetie== on the soft days, and ==jaana== when I mean it most. And sometimes, when no other word is big enough, you are my ==Jind==, which is simply the truest thing I know how to call you.
