After two months of talking every single day, the twenty-first of May finally arrived. This is placeholder text for the story of our first official date, the day that turned *talking* into *us*.

I remember being nervous in the way you are only nervous about things that matter. I changed my shirt twice. I checked the time far too often. And then I saw you, and every bit of the nervousness simply went somewhere else.

![Placeholder for a photo of the table at dinner](../../photos/placeholder-04.jpg "the table, the food, the two of us") ![Placeholder for a photo of us that evening](../../photos/placeholder-05.jpg "before the bill came")

We stayed longer than we needed to. The food was good, but honestly I don't remember much of it, because I was busy trying to memorise your laugh.

When the night ended, I already knew I wanted every next one.
